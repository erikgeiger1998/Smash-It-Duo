Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Zero Tracking

We do not track your location, collect personal identifiers, or monitor your browsing activity.

100% On-Device

All hinge angle and CoreMotion kinematics operate locally in volatile memory and are never transmitted.

No Third-Party Ads

Zero ad tracking SDKs, zero data brokers, and zero monetized user profiling of any kind.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS application "SMASH IT! The Foldable Fly Swatter" (including "SMASH IT! DUO"), developed by Erik Geiger ("we", "our", or "developer"). We believe in absolute user privacy. Our software is designed to be an arcade reflex experience, not an advertising or data harvesting platform.

2. Sensor Data (CoreMotion & Hinge Kinematics)

To detect when your foldable device folds shut, SMASH IT! DUO accesses your device's built-in motion sensors through Apple's official CMMotionManager framework (including gyroscope, accelerometer, and hinge rotation vectoring).

  • Sensor data is processed in real-time strictly in volatile local memory (RAM).
  • Sensor readings are never recorded, saved to disk, or sent across a network.
  • The instant a clap or swat is calculated, motion data is discarded immediately.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

SMASH IT! DUO does NOT collect or process any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses (unless you contact customer support).
  • Precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • Camera or microphone feeds (zero camera or microphone permissions are requested).
  • Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or Vendor ID (IDFV) for marketing purposes.
  • Financial or payment data (all purchases are handled exclusively by Apple via the App Store).

4. Local Data Storage

The game saves your local high scores, current wave progress, and cosmetic settings (such as sound on/off and appearance preferences) directly on your device using standard iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you delete the app.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because SMASH IT! DUO does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple's Kids Category guidelines. We do not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

6. Direct Customer Support Communications

If you voluntarily contact developer support via our support email (support@erikgeiger.ro) or our website contact form, we use your email address and message content solely to reply to your inquiry. We never sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.
